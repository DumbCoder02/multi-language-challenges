Title: Tic-Tac-Toe

Problem Description
Build a console version of Tic-Tac-Toe for two players who share the keyboard. Player X always moves first and the players alternate turns. The game ends when a player fills a complete row, column, or diagonal, or when all nine cells are filled.

The board is a 3 x 3 grid. Rows and columns are numbered 1 to 3, with row 1 at the top and column 1 on the left. An empty cell is shown as a dot (.).

Input Format
On each turn the current player enters two integers separated by a space: the row and the column of the cell to mark. One move per line.

Output Format
Before every turn the board is printed in this layout:

 X | O | .
---+---+---
 . | X | .
---+---+---
 . | . | O

The board is followed by the prompt:
Player X, enter row and column (1-3):

Messages
- Invalid input. Enter two numbers between 1 and 3.
    Printed when the input is not two integers between 1 and 3. The same player is asked again.
- That cell is already taken.
    Printed when the chosen cell is not empty. The same player is asked again.
- Player X wins!   (or Player O wins!)
    Printed after the final board when a player completes a line.
- It's a draw!
    Printed after the final board when all nine cells are filled and nobody has won.

If the input ends before the game is finished, the program exits quietly.

Constraints
- Exactly two players, X and O. X moves first.
- Input is read from standard input.
